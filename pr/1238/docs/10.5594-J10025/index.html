Report of the Television Committee

Abstract
The Television Committee of the Society during the past year has carried out a considerable amount of work as listed: Flicker and visual fatigue in television has been studied, a preliminary report on which work is presented herein. A study of the most suitable type of film for television transmissions has been carried out, a report on which will be presented also. More material has been added to a bibliography and glossary of terms in the field of television, which work was started more than a year ago and which still continues.
