JPEG Status and Progress Report 2022

Abstract
This is an updated report of the work of the JPEG Committee formed by the International Telecommunication Union (ITU), the International Organization for Standardization (ISO), and the International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC), known as the Joint Photographic Experts Group (JPEG). A detailed introduction to JPEG’s work was already presented in Ref. 1. In this report, the focus is more on updated work since 2020 and on new work items and explorations.

Author(s)
Siegfried Foessel
bio
Siegfried Foessel received Diploma and PhD degrees in electrical engineering from Friedrich Alexander University, Erlangen, Germany. He is the head of the Department of Moving Picture Technologies, Fraunhofer IIS, Erlangen, and a professor with the HFF Film School, Munich, Germany. His research projects include digital camera technology, digital cinema, and broadcast workflows, post-production tools, and standardization activities. He is a member and the chair of various national and international standardization bodies. He is a SMPTE Fellow and an IEEE Senior Member with more than 80 publications.
João Ascenso
bio
João Ascenso is a professor with the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Instituto Superior Técnico, University of Lisbon, Lisbon, Portugal, and is with the Multimedia Signal Processing Group, Instituto de Telecomunicações (IT), Lisbon. He is very active in the ISO/IEC MPEG and JPEG standardization activities and currently chairs the JPEG AI ad-hoc group that targets the evaluation and development of learning-based image compression. He has published more than 100 papers in international conferences and journals and has more than 3,400 citations over 35 papers (h-index of 26).
Luis Alberto da Silva Cruz
bio
Luis Alberto Da Silva Cruz received Licenciado and MSc degrees in electrical engineering from the University of Coimbra, Coimbra, Portugal, in 1989 and 1993, respectively. He also holds an MSc degree in mathematics and a PhD degree in electrical computer and systems engineering from the Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute (RPI), Troy, NY, granted in 1997 and 2000, respectively. He has been with the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, University of Coimbra, since 1990, first as a teaching assistant and as an assistant professor since 2000. He is a researcher at the Institute for Telecommunications of Coimbra, where he works on video processing and coding and (bio)medical image processing.
Touradj Ebrahimi
bio
Touradj Ebrahimi is a professor at the École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL), Lausanne, Switzerland, and heads its Multimedia SignalProcessing Group. He is the cofounder of the startup company RayShaper, Crans-Montana, Switzerland, which is active in the computational vision for machine- and human-centric applications. He has been the convener (chairman) of the JPEG Standardization Committee since 2014. His research interests include image and video compression, quality of experience in multimedia, and media security.
Pierre-Anthony Lemieux
bio
Pierre-Anthony Lemieux co-chairs the High-Throughput JPEG 2000 (HTJ2K) activity in the JPEG Committee. He is a partner with Sandflow Consulting, San Mateo, CA, where he works with Hollywood and Silicon Valley-based clients on worldwide standards and the research and development of software. He has been involved in the entertainment technology ecosystem, from content authoring to consumer devices, and is an active contributor to several standards activities and open-source projects. He is a SMPTE Fellow.
Carla Pagliari
bio
Carla Pagliari received a PhD degree in electronic systems engineering from the University of Essex, Colchester, U.K., in 2000. Since 1993, she has been a senior researcher and a professor with the Department of Electrical Engineering, Military Institute of Engineering (IME), Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. Her main research activities and interests are focused on light field signal processing and coding, image and video processing, and computer vision. She is a Senior Member of IEEE and currently the JPEG Pleno AhG on light field chair.
Antonio M. G. Pinheiro
bio
Antonio M. G. Pinheiro is an assistant professor at the Universidade da Beira Interior (UBI), Covilha, Portugal, and a researcher with the Instituto de Telecomunicações (IT), Lisbon, Portugal. He received a BSc (Licenciatura) from IST, Lisbon, in 1988 and a PhD in electronic systems engineering from the University of Essex, Colchester, U.K., in 2002. Currently, his research interests are in image processing, namely multimedia quality evaluation, emerging 3 imaging technologies, and medical image analysis. He is a Portuguese delegate to ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC29, currently involved with JPEG Standardization Committee, the JPEG Communication subgroup chair and participating in the JPEG Pleno standard development. He is a Senior Member of IEEE.
Jon Sneyers
bio
Jon Sneyers received a PhD degree in computer science from KU Leuven, Leuven, Belgium. He created the free lossless image format (FLIF). Since 2016, he has been a senior image researcher with Cloudinary, Santa Clara, CA, a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) technology company that is a market leader in image and video management on the web.
Frederik Temmermanns
bio
Frederik Temmermanns is a researcher with imec-VUB, Brussels, Belgium, and a guest professor with the Vrije Universiteit Brussel (VUB), Brussels. He received a PhD in engineering in 2014. His research focuses on multimedia processing, interoperability, security, and authenticity. He has been involved in several research projects in the medical, mobile, and cultural domains. He is the chair of the JPEG Systems subgroup and is active in JPEG Systems, JPEG fake media, and JPEG NFT. He is also the co-founder of VUB Spinoff Company Universum Digitalis, Brussels, which has been responsible for the realization of renowned projects such as Inside Bruegel (insidebruegel.net) and Closer to Van Eyck (closertovanecyck.be). The latter was awarded a 2019 European Heritage/Europa Nostra Award.
