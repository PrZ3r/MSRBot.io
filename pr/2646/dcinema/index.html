Overview

Background

Digital Cinema (D-Cinema) is a standardized ecosystem for packaging, securing, distributing, and exhibiting motion-imaging content in theatrical environments. It defines interoperable building blocks such as composition metadata, MXF track files for picture and sound, subtitles/captions, packaging and asset identifiers, and a security model for protected content and keys. In practice, D-Cinema workflows connect mastering and distribution deliverables (e.g., DCDM and DCP) with operational requirements in cinemas, enabling consistent playback behavior across equipment vendors and venues.
