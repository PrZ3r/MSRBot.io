More, Faster, Higher, Wider: A Brief History of Increases in Perceptible Characteristics of Motion Imaging

Metadata

Abbreviated Title
SMPTE Mot. Imag. J
Volume
125, No. 6, pp. 32–40
Abstract
Since at least the 19th century, if not earlier, people have tried to improve such characteristics of motion imaging as spatial resolution, aspect ratio, frame rate, dynamic range, and color gamut. Many of those improvements have affected other characteristics. There is no indication that improvements will soon stop.
